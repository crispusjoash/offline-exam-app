Question Text: During a lesson, Grade 6 learners discussed functions of different parts of a plant. Which part of a plant is responsible for making plant food?
Options:
  - Root. (isCorrect: False)
  - Stem. (isCorrect: False)
  - Fruit. (isCorrect: False)
  - Leaf. (isCorrect: True)
Current Explanation:

Question Text: Learners were asked to improvise a triangular bandage. Which one of the following material was suitable for improvising the bandage?
Options:
  - Newspapers. (isCorrect: False)
  - Banana leaves. (isCorrect: False)
  - Pieces of clothes. (isCorrect: True)
  - A piece of carton. (isCorrect: False)
Current Explanation:
A triangular bandage is primarily made from fabric.

* Pieces of old clothes can easily be cut into a triangular shape and are soft, flexible, and strong enough to support injured limbs, unlike paper, leaves, or carton.

Question Number: 50
Question Text: When starting a race in athletics, competitors are given commands. Which of the following command is not given?
Options:
  - Go. (isCorrect: False)
  - Set. (isCorrect: False)
  - Run. (isCorrect: True)
  - On your marks. (isCorrect: False)
Current Explanation:
The standard starting commands for a sprint race are 'On your marks', 'Set', and 'Go' (which is often a starting pistol).

* **'Run'** is not used as a starting command.
