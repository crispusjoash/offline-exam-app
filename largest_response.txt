Here are the specific questions where the official marking scheme answers are highly questionable or logically flawed based on the texts provided:

### 📖 English 2023 Paper
**1. Question 11:**
- **The Question:** The shopkeeper was surprised to see Hassan back because _________
- **Marking Scheme Answer:** A (Hassan returned the money.)
- **Why it's flawed:** The text explicitly says: *"The shopkeeper was surprised to see him back so soon."* This implies she was surprised because of the timeline (i.e., **C. Hassan had just left the shop**). Furthermore, the text separates her surprise from the money: *"When Hassan handed her the money, she was very grateful..."* She was surprised by his quick return, and grateful for the money.

**2. Question 13:**
- **The Question:** Which one of the following is true according to the story?
- **Marking Scheme Answer:** D (Hare buried one of his legs in the ground.)
- **Why it's flawed:** The story never actually says Hare buried one leg. It just says Hare replied, *"My legs are very short."* Conversely, option **A (Hare stole the groundnuts just like Hyena)** is undeniably true, as the very first sentence states: *"Many afternoons, Hyena and Hare spent hours in a field eating the farmer's groundnuts."* 

**3. Question 30:**
- **The Question:** According to the story, who plays football only?
- **Marking Scheme Answer:** A (Nasir.)
- **Why it's flawed:** The text clearly states: *"Nasir, Banda and Lona are friends. They all play football. Nasir also plays volleyball. Lona also plays netball."* Since Nasir plays football *and* volleyball, he cannot be the one who plays "football only". Banda is the only friend who isn't explicitly linked to a second sport, making **B (Banda)** the only logically correct answer.
